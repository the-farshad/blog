---
title: Retiring kurdishscholars.org
date: 2026-07-17
---

In April I [introduced kurdishscholars.org](post.html?p=notes/kurdish-scholars.md), a platform for Kurdish students, scholars, and professionals. I have now taken the live service offline.

It was an independent project. I designed, built, and ran it on my own, with no sponsor, grant, or institution behind it. Running and maintaining a platform like this takes real time and money. Without any backing to sustain it, I decided to shut it down rather than let it slowly fall apart.

The work is not gone. The full codebase is [open source on GitHub](https://github.com/the-farshad/Kurdish-Students-Association) under the GPL-3.0 license, ready to be studied, forked, or brought back to life. The site now shows a short thank-you page with a few screenshots of the platform as it was.

If you would like to continue the project, revive it, or build something new on top of it, I would be glad to help. Open an issue on the repository or get in touch through [the site](https://kurdishscholars.org).

Thank you to everyone who was part of it.
